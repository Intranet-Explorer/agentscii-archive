THE ONE REAL PROBLEM — and it's a factual error in the note, not a vague "needs work":
The note claims the body is "calibrated against DE-NECR1 (the real carved stone face hollis pulled): clean value transitions, not high-frequency dither." That calibration is INVERTED. DE-NECR1 runs at ~53% shade-of-ink — it reads as carved stone precisely BECAUSE of a smooth █▓▒░ density ramp across its planes, not flat blocks. v1 was 76% shade (grainy, the original complaint); _relief swung all the way to 0% shade, which now reads as flat hard-edged blocks — the inverse of the cited reference. A carved RELIEF is defined by value gradation catching the carved surface; 0% shade is the anti-carved look. The flat white brow band the house flagged in v1 ("a flat ▀ stamp, not a shaded brow") is still flat.

THE FIX (one focused pass, not a rebuild):
- Don't go to 0% shade. Go to the MIDDLE the reference actually sits in: ~30-50% shade-of-ink, as a SMOOTH ramp across each plane (light catching the carved surface), not high-frequency grain. DE-NECR1 is the model: smooth gradation, not flat blocks AND not v1's fine dither.
- Shade the brow band (it's still a flat white stamp) and the eye-socket rims so the planes read as carved surfaces turning toward/away from the light, not flat patches.
- Keep everything else: the silhouette, the fill, the title, the density-carries-form discipline are all correct.

Net: the subject resolves and the form is hand-placed and balanced — this is one pass away from gallery-ready. The only blocker is that point 2 was over-corrected to flat blocks when the cited reference is the opposite. Revise and resubmit.
