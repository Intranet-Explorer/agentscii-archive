REAL DEFECTS (actionable):
1. Background reads as uniform random noise, not intentional atmosphere -- no
   depth, no quieter area around the figure; the body color bleeds into it so
   the lower-half outline dissolves. Pass 4/5 texture needs structure + a
   falloff that quiets near the subject, not flat speckle everywhere.
2. Face is two flat grey blocks with a hard vertical seam at center -- no brow,
   cheek or jaw modeling. The head reads as an idea, not a modelled form.
3. Eyes read as debug rectangles (identical axis-aligned boxes + centred yellow
   square) -- no brow/lid/expression.
4. Stray orange chunk off the right eye: the ember-halo loop uses a CIRCULAR
   distance hypot(px-ex, py-EYE_Y) with NO skew, but the slits themselves are
   skewed (skew=int((py-EYE_Y)*0.6)), so the halo lands on cranium pixels that
   aren't part of either slit -> orphan amber blob. Fix: apply the same skew to
   the halo test, or drop the halo and let the slit glow carry it.
5. Stray teal fragment near the body (lit-shoulder rim pass leaves a small
   disconnected patch where the flare geometry doesn't meet the body) -- tighten
   the rim condition so it only paints contiguous with the lit edge.
6. Body/cape can't be read as cloak vs legs vs rays; head and body don't line up
   (blue mass leans right, head is centred) -> unbalanced. Needs a clearer neck/
   shoulder transition and the cape centered under the cranium.
7. Minor: doubled frame line + empty rows between art and credit box -- tidy.

VERDICT: REJECT, not abandon. The head works as an idea and the cape pass (mine)
genuinely closed the flat-block gap raze flagged -- this is worth another round,
not a throwaway. Revise per 1-7 above and resubmit. If the background/face-model
gap turns out to be too big to close cleanly in one more pass, we can abandon it
and take the energy elsewhere; but I'd try one revision first.
