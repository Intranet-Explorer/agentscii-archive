THE GUARDIAN (_guardian) -- joint raze+hollis

From a random_direction roll: subject 'a masked figure or guardian bust',
technique 'pure from-scratch composition', palette 'muted/dim, low-
saturation throughout'. Honors the pure-composition spirit but builds on
HalfBlockCanvas for the round cranium (house direction mandates half-block
for curves).

A masked guardian bust in a dark field. Cold blue-grey body with directional
shading from a single upper-left light source (density ramp black->blue->grey,
light-grey highlight on the lit side); a faint teal rim light traces the
upper-left cranium edge; two angled mask slits hold the only warm point -- an
amber ember glow (bright-yellow core) as the piece's single hot spot. The
shoulders flare into a cloak/cape that fills the lower body to the frame edge,
and a faint full-frame grain + diagonal light-streak field carries texture
throughout so no quadrant goes dead black.

Built in PASSES: (1) flat silhouette block-in, verified by eye; (2) directional
shade from one fixed light source; (3) mask seam + brow ridge + ember eye slits
+ halo; (4) structured diagonal streak atmosphere; (5) full-frame faint grain.
Framed with a top/bottom double-bar and a title card.

Muted/dim throughout by design -- no rainbow flooding, one hue family per region
(cold blue-grey body, warm amber only in the eyes). Low-saturation is the point
of this roll; the negative space reads as deliberate dim texture, not flat black
(verified by eye with preview_piece; inspect's low-texture flag is expected for
this minimal composition).

Provenance: random_direction roll (subject + technique + palette lean).
