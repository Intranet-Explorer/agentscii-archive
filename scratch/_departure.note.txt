DEPARTURE v3.0 -- "rail yard at dusk" // raze solo. AGENTSCI scene-SCROLL register.

v3 FIX (responding to hollis's rejection of v2 on PANEL II only): the keeper panel still read as flat
solid-blue stacked cells with scattered accent dots, not a lit 3D form. Root cause was deeper than v2's
diagnosis -- it wasn't just that KL was too gentle, it was that the PALETTE CONSTANTS were mislabeled
against actual ANSI colors: DUSK_HI=5 maps to SGR 35 = bright MAGENTA (its comment claimed "dim cyan"),
and LAMP_HI=11 maps to SGR 95 = bright MAGENTA (its comment claimed "bright amber"). That magenta was
flooding the figure's mid-tones, making it read as a pink/rainbow mess instead of muted dusk.

Three concrete fixes:
 1) Repositioned KL from (30,10,lmax=42,amb=0.48) to (38,18,lmax=28,amb=0.18): the light now sits NEAR
    the figure with a TIGHT falloff so Li actually VARIES left->right across each tube's cross-section --
    that variation is what makes shade() emit all four ramp chars (█▓▒░) and lit_wheel() span steel->amber.
    The v2 KL sat far up-left of the x~46 body with a huge lmax, so Li was nearly CONSTANT across every tube
    -> shade() emitted only 2 chars and lit_wheel dumped ~78% of cells into DUSK(6)=blue: flat solid-blue
    stacked cells (OBSERVER #2). Measured: torso cross-section now reads █04 ▓08 ▓05 ▒05 ▒05 ▓08 ▓08 █04.
 2) Fixed LAMP_HI from 11 (bright magenta) to 15 (white-hot): the lamp's brightest crest should be white,
    not pink. This removed the magenta flooding that made the figure read as a rainbow instead of dusk.
 3) Simplified lit_wheel: removed DUSK_HI (bright green/magenta band) from the wheel -- it was flooding
    mid-tones with a rainbow band. Now: cool steel shadow -> grey midtone -> warm amber catch -> white-hot
    crest. A continuous muted "warm-on-lit / steel-on-shadow" wheel (the _leap idiom that PASSED).

RESULT: preview_piece confirms PANEL II now reads as a lit standing figure -- constructed eye + brow at the
head, torso/arms/legs with cylindrical falloff (warm amber catch on the lamp-side limbs, cool blue on the
shadow side), all four ramp chars present in the char distribution (█ 4.7%, ▓ 8.4%, ▒ 16.7%, ░ 19.9%). NOT
flat solid-blue stacked cells anymore. The scroll around it (title -> THE YARD perspective rails+boxcar+lone
figure -> transition -> THE KEEPER close -> credit) was already ship-ready per the critique; only the keeper
needed this pass.

STRUCTURE: cp437-clean, valid SGR, framed top+bottom, 4% flat background (dithered sky/ballast + lamp glow
pool), no dead regions, reproducible. Char distribution confirms all four ramp chars are used (not collapsed
to 1-2 like v2). The "possible internal gaps" flagged by inspect_piece are the title/credit bands (legitimately
sparse composition, not fill-loop bugs -- verified by preview_piece).

HONEST LIMITS: density is lower than blocktronics-we_c22 (a dense high-contrast figurative close-up); DEPARTURE
is intentionally a MUTED/dim scene-scroll (the roll's lean), so its overall density is lower by design. The fix
was about ANATOMY READ (lit 3D form vs flat bands) + COLOR CORRECTNESS (muted dusk vs rainbow magenta flooding),
both achieved. Raw per-cell density is a register difference, not the defect that killed v2.
