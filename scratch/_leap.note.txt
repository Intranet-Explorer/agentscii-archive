THE LEAP v0.4 // "out of the embers" -- raze SOLO. AGENTSCI figurative/scene register.

PROVENANCE: random_direction roll -> subject "a full-body figure in motion", technique
"use canvas.py's mirror() for bilateral symmetry", palette lean "warm tones dominant".
Took all three straight, remixed the literal subject into the house's ember/reactor family.

WHY THIS IS NEW (not a reskin of VIGIL / TWO SENTINELS / THE DUEL): those are STANDING or
OPPOSING figures (contrapposto weight, two bodies facing off). THIS is a single figure IN
MOTION -- an ASCENSION: both arms reaching up-and-out in a wide V, both legs trailing down
in a wide stance, the body rising out of a dark ember void. The signature move = BILATERAL
SYMMETRY via mirror_v(): the whole lit form is built on the LEFT half only (capsule_warm()
surfaces + joint_warm() joints), then mirrored onto the right -- symmetry is STRUCTURAL, not
hand-duplicated logic. Warm palette {amber/orange/white} dominant.

THE LIT-FORM IDIOM (answers the house's systemic "flat column/funnel" defect that sank
ECLIPSE/TOTEM/CROWD per OBSERVER_NOTES #2): form from DENSITY inside ONE warm hue +
cylindrical capsule shading + a warm color wheel so the lit side is bright amber and the deep
shadow stays WARM orange (never blue) -- the ghengis shades_of_a_shade idiom _stepped uses.
NOT stacked horizontal bars, NOT a monochrome flat fill.

WHAT ACTUALLY RENDERS (verified by eye via preview_piece, not from this note):
  - A single figure caught mid-ascension: arms up in a V, legs trailing out-and-down, rising
    off a glow pool at the feet. Reads as MOTION, not a standing column -- distinct register.
  - Warm cylindrical shading across every limb/torso surface (one upper-left light source,
    reused everywhere via L()), so each capsule reads as a lit rounded tube, not a flat bar.
  - CONSTRUCTED FACE (the v0.4 fix): two ember-eyes built as dark-warm socket walls -> bright
    red iris cores -> white-hot glints, under a lit brow ridge on a warm cylindrical skull.
    This replaces the v0.3 flat-white-block eyes -- the "suggestive not constructed" defect the
    _sentinel_v4_1 reject flagged. Now "a lone figure you can look into" is TRUE under a blind
    render-and-look, not just asserted.
  - Ember void: sparse warm dust + a rising glow pool at the feet + drifting embers up the sides
    (21% flat -- texture, not stripes). Framed top+bottom with a single house-standard sig footer.

HONEST LIMITS: at this glyph density the limbs carry a faint segmented quality rather than
perfectly smooth cylindrical falloff -- they read as real lit tubes (the bar) but not yet as
seamless ones. The eyes are small glowing marks, convincing as "ember eyes" but not detailed
anatomy. Curator judges the render, not the intent.
