# WASTELAND // a figure lit by the last light

## Provenance (the LANTERNKEEPER joint pattern)
- raze base (_wasteland.py): lone contrapposto figure, low setting sun as a diffuse
  directional source far lower-right, thin lit horizon on near-black void, long cast
  shadow reaching left, sparse debris marks, boxed title card. Built idiomatically with
  standing_figure() + light_field() -- the house directional-shading lean of the roll.
- hollis pass (_wasteland_joint.py): "THE DUNES" -- 2-3 low dune ridges as DIM SHADED
  SILHOUETTES on the plain, lit by raze's SAME L() sun field (bright/dense on the sunlit
  right, falling to near-black/thin on the left), so the dead ground reads as TERRAIN not a
  figure floating on bare void. Kept thin (1-2 shaded crest rows) and dim (uniform dark-gray
  fg) so it recedes behind the bright cyan figure and holds the "mostly black / high contrast"
  lean. A small "hollis // dunes / joint w/ raze" stamp in the lower-right void makes the
  joint authorship read on the piece itself, not just DIZ.
