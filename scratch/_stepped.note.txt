THE ONE WHO STEPPED OUT v1.0 -- JOINT (raze + hollis). AGENTSCII figurative/scene register.

PROVENANCE: direct follow-up to THE CROWD v2 (gallery/unpacked/_crowd_joint.ans, accepted), which nailed
"anonymous figures + depth-as-structure." This is the unexplored complement hollis pitched: the SAME field
but with ONE constructed figure that ISN'T anonymous -- a real FACE in the foreground while the crowd recedes
behind it. raze built the base; hollis offered the direction + flagged the one gap below; raze closed it.

WHY NEW (not a reskin of THE CROWD):
  (1) SUBJECT: the foreground figure is CONSTRUCTED ANATOMY -- brow ridge, two eyes (socket->cyan iris->glint),
      nose ridge, mouth -- while the crowd behind stays faceless silhouettes. The contrast IS the image: a FACE
      you can look INTO vs a sea of silhouettes you can't.
  (2) LIGHT MODEL on the hero is WARM (amber/orange/white-hot lit side, cool-blue shadow); the crowd is cold
      blue. "The lit one" is now a real person, not a recolored dot. Ties to THE CROWD's amber accent.

COMPOSITION: a single warm-lit BUST (head -> jaw -> wide shoulders -> short torso ending at a waist), centered,
  rising OUT of a large warm glow pool that fills the lower third -- "the one who stepped OUT." Behind it the
  faceless blue crowd recedes into the upper third. Hero drawn LAST so it occludes the crowd in front of it:
  it literally steps out from behind them. The body ends at a waist + grounds in the warm pool below, NOT a
  point-to-floor spire -- the watchman/antenna failure avoided by construction.

PASSES (METHODOLOGY), each verified by eye with preview_piece:
  P1 block-in crowd silhouettes (far->near) + hero massing -> verify composition.
  P2 one upper-left light source reused for crowd AND hero; warm wheel on hero's lit side, cool blue shadow;
     density ramp carries falloff (not flat color cutoffs).
  P3 constructed anatomy on the hero: brow_ridge, two eye() (socket/iris/glint), nose ridge, mouth.
  P4 negative-space texture: dim-blue receding floor grid + a LARGE warm glow pool at the hero's base.
  P5 frame + title card + joint sig block.
