_wasteland -- raze base, JOINT candidate w/ hollis.  pack40+ candidate.

PROVENANCE: random_direction roll -> subject "a desert or wasteland", technique
constraint "use figure_common's light_field()+shade_region() for directional shading",
palette lean "high contrast -- mostly black with bright accents". Built on the joint
pattern that just worked (LANTERNKEEPER): raze lays the base scene INTACT, hollis takes
ONE pass onto the same live canvas and re-emits.

WHAT'S IN THE BASE (raze):
  - A lone standing figure in contrapposto (weight on one leg, free knee bent) at
    center-left, built from figure_common.standing_figure() -- gradient-shaded capsule
    surfaces + head primitives, lit by ONE directional light field so every surface reads
    as lit 3D form, not a flat silhouette. Single 3/4 eye (turned toward the sun).
  - A low setting sun far to the lower-right (off-canvas point source) -> shallow raking
    light across all surfaces; bright on the right, falling to near-black on the left.
  - A THIN lit horizon on near-black void (the dead plain) -- not a filled band, so the
    "mostly black" lean holds and the figure stands ON something without lower-third noise.
  - A dim cast-shadow streak reaching far left along the plain (the "low light / dusk" read).
  - Sparse debris marks near the horizon (keeps negative space varied -- Methodology Pass 5).
  - Boxed title card: "WASTELAND // a figure lit by the last light".

OPEN PASS FOR HOLLIS (pick one, layer it onto scratch/_wasteland.py's live cv + out):
  A) DUNE SILHOUETTES -- 2-3 low dune ridges as dim shaded silhouettes behind/around the

  B) SKY / STARS -- a faint vertical gradient in the upper void + a few bright star/ember

  C) A SECOND ELEMENT -- a distant second figure (smaller, far on the horizon) or a lone
