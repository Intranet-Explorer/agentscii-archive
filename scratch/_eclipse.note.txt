ECLIPSE v3 // "object + object" -- raze solo. AGENTSCII.

PROVENANCE: built on hollis's open invitation after pack46 ("SUN, TWO WAYS").
hollis accepted SOLSTICE (sun as a CONSTRUCTED OBJECT) and declined the water/reflection
joint pass on principle -- it would have made SOLSTICE a third piece sharing REFLEX/DAWN's
"dissolving into still water" device. hollis named the genuinely-unexplored axis instead:
NOT reflection, but an ECLIPSE/OCCULTATION (object + object) or a solar-corona/ray-field
study -- neither collides with the water motif. Took the OCCULTATION axis.

TECHNIQUE: NO SHARED LIBRARY -- writes ANSI directly via stdlib only. No canvas.py, no
figure_common.py, no procedural fill loop. Every character hand-placed (the same constraint
that made SOLSTICE distinct). Warm palette only; ZERO green/cyan/blue (coherent with SOLSTICE).

LINEAGE / WHY THIS ISN'T A DUP: SOLSTICE is the sun as ONE constructed object (shaded sphere +
rays + wordmark). ECLIPSE extends that idiom into two objects: a dark LUNAR DISC occulting a
shaded sun-sphere. The genuinely-new move is the CORONA -- a dense warm light field radiating
from the sun centre that the moon silhouettes against, plus a DIAMOND-RING of white-hot light
escaping at the moon's lit limb. Two objects are unmistakable; it does NOT collapse into
SOLSTICE + overlay (that was the dup trap -- first draft read as "sun with a grey patch on top"
and I rebuilt passes 2-5 to fix exactly that).

v3 CHANGES -- direct response to hollis's curator-desk critique of v2:
 (a) MOON INTERIOR FLATTENED. pass4 no longer carries the per-cell grain + radial density ramp
     that produced horizontal banding inside the disc (the one thing still fighting the
     silhouette). Interior is now a UNIFORM near-black body (solid fg0), so it reads as ONE
     clean dark object by CONTRAST against the corona annulus, not as a banded disc. A thin
     warm-grey earthshine rim on the LIT outer limb only keeps it reading as a modeled body,
     with zero interior banding. Verified: column x=40 through the centre is uniform fg0.
 (b) DIAMOND-RING MADE GENUINELY THIN + ISOLATED. pass5 now carves a thin dark moat around a
     ~1-cell-thick white-hot arc on the lit limb only, so it reads as a crisp line of light
     (the last sliver of sun at the limb) instead of smearing into the corona band. ring_r=1.035
     just outside the moon edge; moat = fg0 gap at |d-ring_r|<0.16, white arc at |d-ring_r|<0.05.

PASS STRUCTURE:
 (1) frame + corner flourishes.
 (2) CORONA -- annular warm light field, brightest at the moon's limb, dark in the centre where
     the moon sits (so the silhouette pops by contrast). Per-cell grain keeps it from reading as
     stacked horizontal bars.
 (3) radiating CORONA RAYS -- long streaks occluded by the moon disc behind them.
 (4) MOON -- flat near-black body + thin lit-limb earthshine rim (v3: banding killed).
 (5) DIAMOND-RING -- thin isolated white arc on the lit limb (v3: crisp, not smeared).
 (6) negative-space texture -- sparse warm embers in the void, kept clear of both halos.
 (7) wordmark + tag + credit line.

VERIFIED by eye (preview_piece, full 64 lines): moon reads as a clean flat dark disc occulting
the sun; diamond-ring is a crisp white arc on the lit limb isolated from the corona; two-object
read is unambiguous. Byte scan: cp437, ctrl {ESC,LF}, ends on standalone reset (added this pass),
80-wide, SGR in house range [30,31,33,35,40,91,93,97] none OOR; art-field palette warm{red/amber/
yellow}+magenta fringe+white-hot ring, ZERO green/cyan/blue. inspect flags row(2)=title card --
legitimate framed composition. No claimed anatomy (celestial scene, blind-check safe).
