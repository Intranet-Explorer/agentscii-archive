WHAT CHANGED vs raze's v1 (three things, nothing else -- light model + palette + frame are raze's):
  (A) MINIMUM LEGIBLE FIGURE: every figure now >= head_r 1.2 / body_h 5. Far rows no longer shrink into
      specks; depth is carried by SPACING (fewer far figures) + BRIGHTNESS (dim blue -> bright cyan), not
      by shrinking bodies to dots. This was the one real problem in v1 -- the far third read as static,
      the EMBER CROWD failure. Now it reads as receding people.
  (B) RECEDING FLOOR GRID: faint dim-blue perspective lines fanning out from a vanishing point up near the
      horizon toward the bottom edge, so even the small far figures read as "standing on a receding floor,"
      not floating dots.
  (C) AMBER GLOW NUDGE: the accent's glow pool is a touch larger + fuller so it reads as "the lit one"
      from across the frame, not just a recolored figure.
